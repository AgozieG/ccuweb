Call for Papers

Submissions are invited for International Conference on Emerging Technologies for Multidisciplinary Innovation and Sustainability (ETMIS 2025). This is an exceptional platform for researchers, academics, students, innovators, scientists, and industry practitioners to discuss & fostering innovation and addressing sustainability challenges.

Prospective authors are invited to submit full paper in single column format via the conference website. More information on about paper format and guidelines for paper submission can be found at the conference website.

Submit papers via Microsoft CMT service before October 30, 2025 November 10, 2025.


The proceedings will be submitted for indexing to SCOPUS, Google Scholar and Web of Science.

Scope of the Conference  

ETMIS 2025 invites original research/review papers that focus on, but are not limited to, the following areas:

Track 1: Emerging Technologies and Smart Computing
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Deep Learning and Neural Networks
Internet of Things (IoT) and Cyber-Physical Systems
Quantum Computing and Edge Intelligence
Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technologies
Human-Centric and Cognitive Computing

Track 2: Sustainable Engineering and Resilient Infrastructure
Smart Grids and Renewable Energy Systems
Energy-Efficient and Green Building Technologies
Resilient Transportation and Urban Mobility Systems
Climate-Responsive Civil and Structural Engineering
Environmental Monitoring and Waste Management Solutions
Low-Power Embedded Systems and Sustainable Material Innovations

Track 3: Data Science, Decision Intelligence and Knowledge Systems
Big Data Analytics and Visualization Techniques
Predictive Modeling and Simulation Methods
Explainable AI and Advanced Decision Support Systems
Natural Language Processing and Text Mining
Computational Social Science and Policy Modeling
Knowledge Graph Construction and Reasoning Mechanisms

Track 4: AI Driven Multidisciplinary Innovation for Sustainable Development
AI Applications for Achieving UN SDG Goals
Cybersecurity in Emerging Technology Deployments Across Domains
AI-Driven Innovations in Agriculture, Energy, Climate, and Governance
Ethical, Fair, and Responsible AI for Cross-Sector Systems
Digital Transformation for Rural and Societal Empowerment
Smart Cities and Community-Centric Sustainable Solutions

Track 5: Emerging Technologies in Health Sciences and Education
AI and IoT in Healthcare and Biomedical Applications
Smart Diagnostics, Medical Imaging, and Clinical Decision Support Tools
Wearable and Remote Patient Health Monitoring Systems
AI Enhanced Learning Platforms and Educational Technology Innovations
Virtual, Augmented, and Mixed Reality in Medical Training and Education
Digital Transformation Strategies in Public Health and Academic Delivery

About Conference

The aim of ETMIS 2025 is to provide a premier interdisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and policy-makers to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, and practical challenges encountered in the fields of emerging technologies and their role in fostering sustainable development. This conference emphasizes the importance of cross-disciplinary collaboration in solving real-world problems by leveraging advancements in science, engineering, computing, environmental studies, and management.
